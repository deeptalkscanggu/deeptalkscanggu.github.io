Session Utilities

Tools

Randomiser

Put in entries and get a random output. Sometimes we'll use a randomiser to determine what we talk about. This could be with:

  • Hot Takes — everyone submits a hot take (controversial opinion) and we pick one at random
  • Quotes — we'll pick a random quote from a list
  • Words or WDYM (What Do You Mean?) — we'll pick a random word from a list
Go to Randomiser

QR Codes

Enter links to show QR codes to the group if they need to visit a webpage or form for the Deep Talks discussion. Supports up to four links at once, displayed in a fullscreen grid.
